FOCO QUE VIRA FOLHAS

TEMPO DE TELA HONESTO

• As regras chegam ao celular da criança mesmo com o app fechado.

NOVE COMPANHEIROS
Baru, a capivara; Rio, a lontra; Toco, a tartaruga; Nina, a coruja. Mais cinco para conquistar na trilha. Nenhum é vendido.

UMA TRILHA DE 22 PASSOS

Phone down. Watch it grow.

Baru is a little companion that lives on your focus sessions. Every minute away from the screen turns into leaves, items and a habitat that changes with the time of day. And if you slip? It just misses you. It never dies, never loses anything, never blames you.

FOCUS THAT TURNS INTO LEAVES
• Sessions from 25 to 90 minutes.
• Every finished session earns leaves and XP. Giving up halfway costs nothing: it just doesn't earn.

HONEST SCREEN TIME
• Built on iPhone Screen Time: you choose which apps count and set your daily goal.
• Over your goal? If you want, the chosen apps pause until tomorrow, with your companion on screen.
• The daily report shows time per app, computed on the device.

FAMILY MODE
• One app, three ways: for me, for my child's phone, or as a parent.
• One goal for school days and another for weekends.
• Screen-free hours (night, school), extra time with one tap and instant pause.
• Alerts when the goal gets close or runs out, plus a weekly summary.
• Everything also on meubaru.com, in the parent dashboard.
• Rules reach your child's phone even with the app closed.

NINE COMPANIONS
A capybara, an otter, a tortoise and an owl to start, and five more to unlock along the path. None of them is for sale.

A PATH OF 22 STEPS
From your first focus session to your hundredth. Every milestone has a clear goal and a concrete reward. A step you've earned is never taken back.

SHORT MISSIONS
Three missions a day and two a week, with exact rewards. Expiring at midnight never punishes anyone.

A HABITAT THAT'S YOURS
Leaves become items that drop into the scene. At 10 pm the moon rises and the water changes color. And you can pet your companion.

YOUR DATA
• From screen time, only the daily total leaves your phone. The per-app detail stays on the iPhone.
• The app never reads messages, screen content or what you type.
• "Delete my data", under Settings → Your account, erases everything, on the device and on the server.

In English, Portuguese, Spanish and Chinese.
